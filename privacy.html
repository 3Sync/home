Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

In short

ThreeSync does not collect, store or receive any personal data, whether from this website or from our Roblox models. Some of our models save player stats inside the game that uses them. That data belongs to the game's owner, and we never see it.

This website

This site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or tracking. It is a static page and does not ask for or store any information about you.

Like any website, it relies on a few third-party services that may process basic technical data, such as your IP address, when your browser requests a page:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface used on this site. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to Roblox and Discord take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Our Roblox models

Our models run entirely inside the Roblox experience they are added to. They do not send any data to ThreeSync or to any outside server.

Some models need to keep player data to work. This data stays within that experience on Roblox's platform and is controlled by the experience's owner, not by ThreeSync:

  • Time Played Board: saves a player's Roblox user ID and total time played to the experience's DataStores, used to rank the most active players.
  • Donation Board: saves a player's Roblox user ID and total amount donated to the experience's DataStores, used to rank top donors and award chat tags. Purchases are processed by Roblox, and no payment details are visible to the model or to us.
  • Admin System: does not save anything to DataStores. Bans that last beyond a single server use Roblox's built-in Ban API, so Roblox stores and manages them for that experience. Server-only actions are held in that server's memory and are cleared when the server shuts down.

If you use our models in your game

You are responsible for the data your experience stores, including data saved by our models. This includes acting on Roblox Right to Erasure requests by removing the affected player's data from your experience's DataStores, as required by the Roblox Terms of Use.

If you play a game that uses our models

ThreeSync has no access to your data in that game. For questions about it, or to have it removed, contact the game's owner, or see the Roblox Privacy and Cookie Policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If you have a question, reach us on our Discord server.
